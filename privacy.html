Privacy note

Lobos Training Lab, for Bayou City Lobos families and coaches. Last updated October 2026.

Lobos Training Lab is a private training app for Bayou City Lobos. Coaches use it for session plans, and players complete daily training reps set by their coach. This note explains what we collect and why.

Who holds the account

A parent or guardian creates the account and signs in with their own email or Google account. Children do not sign up themselves.

What we store about your child

Only their first name and last initial, their team, and a record of which training reps they complete. We do not collect a date of birth or age, photograph, school, address, phone number, email address, or location.

What we store about you

Your email address, used only to sign you in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google for that purpose and nothing else.

Who can see it

Club coaching staff can see your child's name, team and training activity. Families on the same team can see your child's first name, last initial and training points on the team leaderboard. We do not sell, share, or disclose any of this to anyone outside the club.

Approval

A new player does not appear on a roster until a club coach approves them.

Older players

At the club's discretion, players aged 13 and over may be given their own login. The parent or guardian keeps full access and can turn that login off at any time.

Your choices

You can correct your child's name or remove your child at any time from the Family tab in the app, which permanently deletes their training records. To delete your whole account, contact the club.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will tell families before the change takes effect.
